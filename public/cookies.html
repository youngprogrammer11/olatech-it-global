Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 2026

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. They help the website work properly and help us understand how visitors use it. This page lists every cookie our website uses, honestly and in full.

Cookies we use

Analytics cookies (Google Analytics). These help us count visits and see which pages are popular, so we can improve the site. They do not identify you personally.

  • _ga — distinguishes one visitor from another. Lasts up to 2 years.
  • _ga_KZBN2KDMN6 — keeps track of your visit session. Lasts up to 2 years.

Essential cookie (admin area only). When the site owner signs in to our admin area, the server sets a session cookie so the sign in is remembered while working. Regular visitors never receive this cookie, and it disappears when the browser is closed or the session ends.

Your cookie notice choice. When you tap Accept on our cookie notice, we store that choice in your browser's local storage (which works like a cookie) so the notice does not keep appearing. It stays there until you clear your browser data.

What we do not use

We do not use advertising cookies. We do not use social media tracking pixels. Nothing on this website follows you to other websites to show you adverts.

How to control cookies

You are in control. You can block or delete cookies at any time in your browser settings. Every major browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge) has a cookies section under its privacy settings. Blocking cookies will not stop this website from working, though Google Analytics will no longer count your visit.

You can also opt out of Google Analytics on every website by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Changes to this policy

If we ever add a new cookie, we will list it on this page and update the date at the top. We encourage you to check this page occasionally.
